CleaningOS V1.8 — Equal Share Staff Reports

Open index.html in a browser. In Reports, choose a date and view Cleaner-wise Work Report and Individual Cleaner Job & Amount Details.

Each non-cancelled booking is divided equally across its assigned cleaners, with AED cents distributed deterministically so shares add to the booking total. Completed value includes only Completed bookings. These values are allocated company revenue, NOT staff wages or collected cash.

Local browser prototype only: data is stored in localStorage, not synchronized or secured for production. Back up data regularly.
